## Start with the manual

Service timing depends on the make, model, engine and how the car is used. Check the owner's manual for the schedule that applies to your exact vehicle. If time and distance limits are both listed, follow the manual's instructions for whichever comes first.

For example, [Hyundai's maintenance guidance](https://www.hyundai.com/in/en/connect-to-service/maintenance) describes regular maintenance and directs owners to the relevant manual. Its [service calculator](https://www.hyundai.com/in/en/connect-to-service/service-calculator) gives indicative estimates by model, fuel, city and service interval. Treat a calculator result as an estimate and confirm the work and price with the authorised workshop.

## Keep the useful details together

After each visit, save the invoice and note the date, odometer reading, work completed and next service due. Keep separate estimates for repairs that were suggested but not approved. That makes it easier to compare the next visit with what was actually done.

## Before you leave

- Check the job card against the work you approved.
- Ask for replaced parts or an explanation if a repair was unexpected.
- Keep the final invoice and warranty documents together.
- Add a reminder using the time or distance interval in your vehicle's manual.

An authorised workshop can confirm the schedule for your exact variant and driving conditions. This guide is general information, not a replacement for the owner's manual.
