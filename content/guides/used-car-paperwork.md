## Before money changes hands

Check that the registration certificate details match the vehicle and the person selling it. Ask to see the original documents, confirm the insurance is current, and arrange an independent inspection. Do not rely on a photo of a document or a verbal promise to complete a transfer later.

## Complete the ownership transfer

The exact process depends on the vehicle and circumstances. India's [Parivahan guidance on transfer of ownership](https://mparivahan.parivahan.gov.in/mstatic/english/rc-info-ownership.html) and the official [transfer forms](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/en/content/download-forms) explain the documents and applications involved. Follow the instructions for the relevant registering authority and keep proof that the application was submitted.

Contact the insurer about transferring or replacing the policy. Confirm when cover takes effect and what documents the insurer needs; do not assume the seller's policy automatically protects a new owner.

## Keep a handover folder

- Signed sale receipt and payment record.
- Registration and ownership-transfer application details.
- Insurance policy and insurer confirmation.
- Pollution-under-control certificate, where applicable.
- Inspection report, service invoices and both keys.

Rules and required documents can change, so check the current Parivahan and insurer instructions for your case before completing the sale.
