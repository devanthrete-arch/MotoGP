# Vehicle Catalogue and 3D Asset Scope

The India lineup in `src/catalogue/indiaLineup.ts` contains 183 model entries checked against the manufacturers' India product pages on 10 October 2026. It covers the currently presented model ranges for Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai, Tata, Mahindra, Hero, Honda, TVS, Bajaj and Royal Enfield. Maruti Suzuki's Tour commercial range is included. Honda's entries marked `pre-booking` are not presented as generally available.

This is a current model-name catalogue, not a history of discontinued vehicles or a complete trim, engine, year, generation or variant database. The separate verified comparison catalogue continues to supply its existing source-backed variants. Recheck the linked manufacturer pages before using this list for availability, pricing, specifications or purchase advice.

## 3D Asset Policy

- Vehicle previews are procedural Three.js geometry rendered in-browser with React Three Fiber and WebGL. They are generic car, motorcycle or scooter concepts, not replicas of named products.
- No manufacturer logo, badge, livery, model-specific silhouette, manufacturer photo, third-party 3D mesh, texture pack or remote model viewer is used by this catalogue preview.
- Catalogue maker and model names are text labels identifying products; each source link points to the corresponding official maker website.
- The landing fallback illustration is CSS authored in this repository. The previous vehicle poster is no longer referenced by the app.
- This repository has no root `LICENSE` file. The absence of imported 3D assets does not itself grant a license to the whole application or resolve trademark questions around factual model names. Obtain a deliberate project license and legal review before making a broad license-free or commercial-rights claim.
