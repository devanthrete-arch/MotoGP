# Autoflex community rules

Autoflex is built for useful ownership-detail culture, but the MVP should feel
fast, recoverable, and easy to moderate.

## Posting standard

- Share real ownership experience when possible: variant, city, fuel type,
  odometer, bill ranges, photos, route, or workshop context.
- Add model, variant, city, and odometer when the post is about a specific car.
  These details power the Models hub.
- Choose the closest post type: owner note, review, known issue, fix, cost
  note, or travelogue. This helps model pages surface useful patterns.
- Use "Worked for me" only when you personally tried a Fix post and it solved
  the issue on your car.
- Mark a post Helpful when it genuinely helps; mark Stale info when the detail
  looks outdated or model-year dependent.
- Mark guesses clearly. Dealer talk, hearsay, leaks, and early news should not
  look like firsthand proof.
- Help the next owner make a better decision.
- No abuse, spam, fake leads, or brand-war bait.

## Moderation standard

- Users can report posts for moderator review.
- Admins can dismiss handled reports.
- Admins can remove abusive posts.
- Admins can pin or unpin important reported posts for model notebooks.
- Admins can block the browser/profile token behind a reported post from future
  community writes.

Service-center integration remains separate under `/api/service-centers/*`.
